## Thesis

**Follow the trace from field friction to durable proof.**

The journal is an editorial record of work in motion. Its first viewport is a verb, its dates and states are checkable facts, and every visual flourish must help a reader understand what changed.

## Custom object

The **ACE Evidence Core** is part geological core sample, part service record, and part lab instrument. Each investigation adds a dated band. The homepage shows accumulated inquiry; an entry shows the evidence available at that moment. It is never a glowing orb or decorative 3D gadget.

## Three show-pass plates

### A — Black-Box Field Portrait

Cinematic documentary still. A used physical Evidence Core sits in a dark graphite field under hard sidelight. Marks, bands, and one continuous teal trace show accumulated work. The object owns the opening viewport.

### B — Dual-System Cutaway

One shared Evidence Core silhouette holds two physically distinct states: source-system reality and operational record. Registration and misalignment make cross-system agreement or drift visible. This direction is legal only when the comparison itself is the subject.

### C — Investigators' Atlas

The Evidence Core is flattened into a contemporary specimen plate with measured cutaways, contact sheets, dated folio leaves, and corrections. Warm technical paper and restrained ink make ongoing research—not nostalgia—the material of the house.

Launch decision: **C informs the journal system; every generated cover candidate is withheld.** Plate A collapsed into a dark cyber-schematic. Plate D became a bookbinding jig with a stepped trace. Plate E finally read as a physical archive, but its ribbon remained visibly disconnected, its beige paper missed the limestone material law, and the object entered the typography field. The journal therefore launches type-first rather than converting a failed acceptance gate into taste by assertion.
