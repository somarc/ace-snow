## Image brief — Black-Box Field Portrait

Create an original cinematic documentary still for an enterprise technical field journal. The sole object is the **ACE Evidence Core**: a slender physical cylindrical archive, part geological core sample, part service record, part precision lab instrument. It has many clean dated-looking bands without any readable numbers or letters. Some layers are warm limestone paper, some blackened navy metal, some smoked glass; all show subtle scratches, handling, test tape, and accumulated use.

One continuous deep-teal trace enters at the oldest end, crosses every band without breaking, passes through two very small amber review gates, and reaches a crisp resolved end. The trace is the only luminous element. Hard restrained sidelight reveals material depth against a nearly black graphite field. The object feels photographed after real work, not rendered as a fantasy device.

Compose the core and strongest light toward the upper-right. Preserve a broad, calm, low-detail navy-black field across the left 40 percent and lower-left for large editorial typography. Use the palette as material, not UI color: ink `#17233b`, limestone paper `#f5f3eb`, deep teal `#0f7f76`, and a very small amber `#f2a43a`. Tactile high-end CGI or studio product photography, subtle film grain, precise calm geometry.

## Visual law

Exactly one cumulative object and exactly one unbroken teal trace must remain obvious at thumbnail size.

## Avoid

Glowing orb, robot, neural network, AI brain, circuit-board cliché, server rack, blockchain coin, cloud icon, purple cyberpunk, generic dashboard, clean unused luxury product, fantasy geology, excessive bloom, or busy background.

- One unmistakable focal object at thumbnail size.
- Left 35–45 percent remains type-safe under desktop and mobile cover crops.
- Material bands read as accumulated history rather than decoration.
- The teal trace is continuous and unique.
- No accidental glyphs, pseudo-text, logos, or interface fragments.
