## Image brief — Dual-System Cutaway

Create an original museum-grade technical cutaway for an enterprise co-innovation journal. The sole object is the **ACE Evidence Core**, shown as one exact shared silhouette containing two physically distinct but aligned states. The upper/near state is source-system reality: warm limestone paper, navy ink, mineral layers, and tangible evidence fragments. The lower/far state is operational record: smoked glass, precise dark metal, and measured archival channels. They are not two separate objects and not two corporate logos.

Every accumulated band crosses both states. A narrow registration seam reveals where layers align and where one or two layers visibly drift. One continuous deep-teal trace traverses both states; two restrained amber registration marks identify review points without symbols or text. The cutaway should make reconciliation between two system truths perceptible in one glance.

Use a pale mineral field with ink-black negative space and a quiet editorial composition. Place the core slightly right of center with a broad low-detail safe zone on the left for typography. Palette: warm paper `#f5f3eb`, ink `#17233b`, deep teal `#0f7f76`, restrained amber `#f2a43a`. Contemporary precision model photography or tactile CGI, clean shadows, no glow spectacle.

## Visual law

One silhouette, two material states, one continuous trace. The comparison must read as system reconciliation—not a before/after marketing split.

## Avoid

Split-screen UI, yin-yang symbol, mirrored faces, corporate logos, glowing orb, robot, AI brain, circuit board, dashboard, purple cyberpunk, floating windows, readable labels, excessive arrows, or generic infographic styling.

## Acceptance tests

- The shared silhouette is legible at thumbnail size.
- Both states remain physically distinct without becoming separate objects.
- Misalignment is visible but not catastrophic or chaotic.
- Left 35–45 percent remains type-safe under cover crops.
- No accidental glyphs, pseudo-text, logos, or interface fragments.
