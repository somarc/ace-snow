## Image brief — Investigators' Atlas

Create an original top-down editorial still for a living enterprise technical journal. The **ACE Evidence Core** has been carefully opened and flattened into a contemporary specimen plate on warm limestone technical paper. Show one measured longitudinal cutaway, one restrained rubbing or contact impression, and several thin chronological folio leaves receding beneath it. The leaves carry abstract ruled divisions, corrections, clipped evidence fragments, and unreadable registration marks—never actual text.

One continuous deep-teal trace runs from the oldest underlying leaf, through the opened core, across two small amber review gates, and exits at the newest clean sheet. The composition should feel actively used and exact: archival margins, graphite handling marks, smoked-glass measuring edges, navy ink, subtle paper fibres, and one or two precise corrections. Contemporary field research, not faux-vintage parchment or a scrapbook.

Cluster the specimen plate and layered evidence toward the right and upper right. Preserve a broad calm paper-and-ink safe zone across the left 40 percent for editorial typography. Palette: limestone paper `#f5f3eb`, ink `#17233b`, deep teal `#0f7f76`, restrained amber `#f2a43a`. Tactile printmaking detail combined with precise contemporary product photography, soft directional daylight, crisp hierarchy, no decorative clutter.

## Visual law

The accumulated record is the object: one core, many dated-looking layers, one unbroken trace from old evidence to current decision.

## Avoid

Faux-antique parchment, sepia nostalgia, mood board, scrapbook, detective corkboard, red string, readable handwriting, maps with labels, botanical specimen, fantasy alchemy, AI brain, circuits, dashboard, purple glow, or busy collage.

## Acceptance tests

- The opened core remains the clear focal object at thumbnail size.
- Chronological layers read as accumulated work, not decorative paper.
- Left 35–45 percent remains type-safe under cover crops.
- The teal trace is continuous and unique.
- No accidental glyphs, pseudo-text, logos, or interface fragments.
