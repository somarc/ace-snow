## Image brief — Open Evidence Archive

Create an original editorial studio photograph for the ACE × ServiceNow enterprise co-innovation field journal. Show one unmistakably physical **ACE Evidence Core**: an opened, precision-made archival cassette holding a deep stack of thick blank evidence leaves. It is part engineering field notebook, part museum conservation box, and part laboratory record—not a computer, machine, or science-fiction device.

The cassette uses warm limestone paper, indigo bookcloth, brushed graphite metal, and one clear smoked-acrylic registration edge. The blank leaves have visibly different thicknesses, deckled edges, clipped corners, compression, fingerprints, and restrained wear so they read as accumulated work across time. Several leaves are gently separated to expose the depth of the record, while the object remains one coherent archive.

Exactly one continuous **matte deep-teal physical inlay** enters beneath the oldest leaves, passes through the whole stack, crosses one tiny amber review stop, and terminates at the newest clean leaf. It is a solid embedded strip or thread, never a beam, glow, circuit, graph, arrow, or decorative flourish. Make the teal path thick and high-contrast enough to remain obvious when the image is only 160 pixels wide.

Place the entire archive in the right 52–56 percent of the frame, photographed from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle. Preserve the left 44–48 percent as an uninterrupted, low-detail navy-black bookcloth field for large editorial typography. No part of the object or its shadow may enter the left 38 percent. Use restrained warm side light, tactile macro detail, subtle film grain, and calm contemporary editorial art direction. The image should feel photographed after difficult collaborative work, not rendered as a luxury gadget.

Material palette: ink navy `#17233b`, limestone paper `#f5f3eb`, matte deep teal `#0f7f76`, and one very small amber `#f2a43a` checkpoint.

## Visual law

One physical archive, many accumulated blank layers, one unbroken non-glowing teal trace from old evidence to current decision.
