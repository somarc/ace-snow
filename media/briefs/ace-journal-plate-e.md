## Image brief — The Open Field Archive

The one focal object is an unmistakable **open archival clamshell case** used to preserve a living technical record. It must read instantly as a conservation-grade document archive: a hinged indigo-bookcloth case, a precisely fitted stack of thick blank limestone evidence cards, and a shallow graphite-metal indexing tray. Do not make a sculpture, model, machine, bookbinding jig, tool, swatch fan, or abstract paper construction.

The case is open toward the camera at a slightly elevated three-quarter angle. Several blank cards are offset only enough to expose real chronological depth, deckled edges, restrained fingerprints, corner wear, and compression from repeated handling. The archive is used but calm, exact, and contemporary—warm neutral limestone rather than beige, brown, sepia, or antique parchment.

One **single, fully visible, uninterrupted woven deep-teal ribbon** lies on top of the cards. The ribbon enters at the oldest card, follows one gentle continuous curve across the entire open stack, passes through exactly one tiny amber metal keeper, and ends on the newest card. Every millimetre of the ribbon must remain visibly connected in the photograph. It must be a tactile textile ribbon—not repeated bands, page-edge color, a staircase, beam, circuit, graph, arrow, or hidden inlay. Make it wide and saturated enough to be unmistakable at 160 × 90 pixels.

Place the complete clamshell case inside the right 48 percent of the frame, with its salient bounds between x=50% and x=95%. Preserve the left 48 percent as uninterrupted, low-detail ink-navy bookcloth for editorial typography. No object, shadow, seam, texture change, or ribbon may enter the left 46 percent. Keep the full case within the frame with generous breathing room above and below so it survives a full-ratio mobile presentation without feeling cramped.

Use soft directional daylight, restrained warm highlights, tactile macro detail, and subtle film grain. Material palette: ink navy `#17233b`, neutral limestone `#f5f3eb`, saturated deep teal `#0f7f76`, and one tiny amber `#f2a43a` keeper.
